# European Banking Authority - Guidelines on ML/TF Risk Factors (EBA/GL/2021/02)

**Jurisdiction:** European Union / UK Reference Standard  
**Document ID:** DOC-EBA-GL-2021  
**Classification:** Public Regulation  
**Effective Date:** 2021-09-21  
**Permitted Roles:** ["analyst", "manager", "auditor"]  

---

## Title I: General Risk Assessment

### Clause EBA-GL-1.12 (Customer and Country Risk Weighting)
Firms must identify and assess money laundering and terrorist financing risks associated with their customers, countries or geographic areas of operation, products, services, transactions, and delivery channels. Risk factor weighting must be documented in a comprehensive Business-Wide Risk Assessment (BWRA) updated at least annually.

### Clause EBA-GL-1.25 (Non-Face-to-Face Onboarding Safeguards)
Where business relationships are established without physical face-to-face interaction, firms must apply compensating technological and operational controls, such as biometric liveness detection and cryptographic verification of identity documents, to mitigate heightened impersonation and synthetic identity risks.

## Title II: Sectoral Guidelines for Retail and Correspondent Banking

### Clause EBA-GL-8.4 (Correspondent Banking Enhanced Diligence)
Before entering into a cross-border correspondent banking relationship, respondent institutions must undergo enhanced due diligence. Firms must evaluate the respondent bank's AML/CFT control environment, confirm that the respondent is not a shell bank, and obtain approval from the management board prior to account opening.
