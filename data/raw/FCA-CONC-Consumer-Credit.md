# Financial Conduct Authority - Consumer Credit Sourcebook (CONC)

**Jurisdiction:** United Kingdom (FCA)  
**Document ID:** DOC-FCA-CONC-2024  
**Classification:** Public Regulation  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01  
**Permitted Roles:** ["analyst", "manager", "auditor"]  

---

## Chapter 5: Responsible Lending and Creditworthiness Assessment

### Clause CONC-5.2.1R (Creditworthiness and Affordability Assessment)
Before entering into a regulated credit agreement or significantly increasing credit limits, a firm must undertake a reasonable assessment of the customer's creditworthiness. The assessment must evaluate the customer's ability to repay the credit without undue difficulty and without incurring adverse consequences for their overall financial stability.

### Clause CONC-5.2.3G (Proportionality of Information Gathering)
The extent and scope of information gathered for creditworthiness assessments must be proportionate to the value of the credit, the repayment terms, and the vulnerability risk profile of the applicant. Firms must not rely exclusively on self-declared customer income when evidence suggests financial distress.

## Chapter 7: Arrears, Default and Recovery

### Clause CONC-7.3.1R (Fair Treatment of Customers in Arrears)
A firm must treat customers in default or in arrears difficulties with forbearance and consideration. A firm must allow a customer reasonable time and opportunity to submit repayment proposals and negotiate sustainable debt arrangement schedules before commencing formal legal debt recovery.

### Clause CONC-7.3.4R (Suspension of Enforcement During Repayment Negotiations)
Where a customer provides evidence of debt management assistance or proposes an affordable repayment plan, a firm must suspend active enforcement action, interest fee accumulation, and external debt collection agency referrals for a minimum breathing space period of 30 days.

## Chapter 8: Debt Advice and Transparency

### Clause CONC-8.3.2R (Free Debt Advice Referrals)
When a firm contacts a customer who is experiencing financial difficulty or persistent arrears, it must inform the customer of the availability of free, independent, and impartial money advice services (such as Citizens Advice, StepChange Debt Charity, or MoneyHelper) before executing any account closure.
