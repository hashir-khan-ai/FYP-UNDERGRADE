**Jurisdiction:** United Kingdom  
**Document ID:** DOC-UK-MLR-2017  
**Classification:** Public Regulation  
**Effective Date:** 2017-06-26 (as amended 2023)  
**Permitted Roles:** ["analyst", "manager", "auditor"]

## Part 3: Customer Due Diligence (CDD)

### Clause MLR-REG-27(1) (Application of Customer Due Diligence)
A relevant person must apply customer due diligence measures whenever the relevant person:
(a) establishes a business relationship;
(b) carries out an occasional transaction that amounts to 15,000 euros or more, whether the transaction is executed in a single operation or in several operations which appear to be linked;
(c) suspects money laundering or terrorist financing; or
(d) doubts the veracity or adequacy of documents or information previously obtained for the purposes of identification or verification.

### Clause MLR-REG-28(2) (Beneficial Ownership Threshold)
For the purposes of customer due diligence, identification of beneficial ownership requires establishing the identity of any natural person who ultimately owns or controls more than 25% of the shares or voting rights in a corporate customer, or who exercises control over the management.

### Clause MLR-REG-33(1) (Enhanced Customer Due Diligence - EDD)
A relevant person must apply enhanced customer due diligence measures and enhanced ongoing monitoring in any of the following cases:
(a) in any transaction or business relationship involving a person established in a high-risk third country;
(b) where a customer is a Politically Exposed Person (PEP), or a family member or known close associate of a PEP;
(c) where a transaction is complex, unusually large, or presents no apparent economic or lawful purpose.

### Clause MLR-REG-35(5) (Approval for PEP Relationships)
A firm must obtain senior management approval before establishing or continuing a business relationship with a Politically Exposed Person (PEP).
