# Terrorism Act 2000 and Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018

**Jurisdiction:** United Kingdom  
**Document ID:** DOC-UK-TACT-2000  
**Classification:** Public Regulation  
**Effective Date:** 2000-07-20 (as amended 2023)  
**Permitted Roles:** ["analyst", "manager", "auditor"]  

---

## Part 3: Terrorist Property and Disclosure Duties

### Clause TACT-SEC-19 (Duty to Disclose in the Course of Business)
A person who believes or suspects that another person has committed an offence under sections 15 to 18 of the Terrorism Act 2000, based on information which comes to their attention in the course of a trade, profession, business or employment, must disclose that information to a constable, or to the designated Nominated Officer, as soon as reasonably practicable.

### Clause TACT-SEC-21A (Failure to Disclose: Regulated Sector Offence)
A person in the regulated financial sector commits a statutory criminal offence if they know, suspect, or have reasonable grounds for knowing or suspecting, that another person is engaged in terrorist financing, and fail to make the required disclosure to the National Crime Agency (NCA) or Nominated Officer as soon as is practicable.

## Part 4: Financial Sanctions Implementation

### Clause SAMLA-SEC-15 (Asset Freezing Obligations)
Where a person or entity is designated under UK financial sanctions regulations, financial institutions must immediately freeze all funds, financial assets, and economic resources owned, held or controlled by the designated person. Institutions must report all frozen assets to the Office of Financial Sanctions Implementation (OFSI) without delay.
