# Financial Conduct Authority - Market Conduct Sourcebook (MAR)

**Jurisdiction:** United Kingdom (FCA)  
**Document ID:** DOC-FCA-MAR-2024  
**Classification:** Public Regulation  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01  
**Permitted Roles:** ["analyst", "manager", "auditor"]  

---

## Chapter 1: Market Abuse and Inside Information

### Clause MAR-1.2.1R (Prohibition of Insider Dealing and Unlawful Disclosure)
A person must not engage or attempt to engage in insider dealing, recommend that another person engage in insider dealing, or unlawfully disclose inside information. Inside information comprises information of a precise nature which has not been made public relating directly or indirectly to financial instruments and which, if made public, would be likely to have a significant effect on prices.

### Clause MAR-1.3.1R (Prohibition of Market Manipulation)
A person must not engage in or attempt to engage in market manipulation, including entering into a transaction or placing an order to trade which gives, or is likely to give, false or misleading signals as to the supply of, demand for, or price of a financial instrument, or secures the price at an artificial level.

## Chapter 8: Benchmarks Administration and Oversight

### Clause MAR-8.3.1R (Governance and Integrity of Benchmark Submissions)
A benchmark submitter must establish and maintain an effective governance framework to identify, prevent and manage conflicts of interest, ensuring that submitters are operationally segregated from trading personnel whose remuneration or performance is tied to benchmark-linked instruments.
