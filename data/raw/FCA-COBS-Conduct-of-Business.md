# Financial Conduct Authority - Conduct of Business Sourcebook (COBS)

**Jurisdiction:** United Kingdom (FCA)  
**Document ID:** DOC-FCA-COBS-2024  
**Classification:** Public Regulation  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01  
**Permitted Roles:** ["analyst", "manager", "auditor"]  

---

## Chapter 4: Communicating with Clients

### Clause COBS-4.2.1R (Fair, Clear and Not Misleading Communications)
A firm must ensure that a communication or a financial promotion is fair, clear and not misleading. This overarching standard applies to all marketing literature, digital client portals, investment research notes, and fee disclosures provided to retail and professional clients.

---

## Chapter 6: Information About the Firm and Its Services

### Clause COBS-6.1.4R (Pre-Investment Costs and Charges Disclosure)
A firm must provide a client with comprehensive information about all costs and associated charges in good time before the provision of investment services. The disclosure must aggregate all costs of investment advice, financial instruments, third-party custody fees, and any foreign exchange mark-ups, presented both as a cash sum and as a percentage.

## Chapter 9: Suitability and Suitability Reports

### Clause COBS-9.2.1R (Assessing Suitability)
A firm must obtain from the client such information as is necessary to understand the essential facts about the client and to have a reasonable basis for believing that the specific transaction meets the client's investment objectives, financial situation including capacity for loss, and knowledge and experience in the investment field.

### Clause COBS-9.4.1R (Requirement to Provide a Suitability Report)
A firm must provide a retail client with a written suitability report explaining why the recommendation has been made, how the investment aligns with the client's risk appetite, and why the firm concluded the transaction is suitable before the contract is concluded.

## Chapter 11: Best Execution and Order Allocation

### Clause COBS-11.2.1R (Obligation to Execute Orders on Terms Most Favourable to the Client)
A firm must take all sufficient steps to obtain, when executing orders, the best possible result for its clients taking into account price, costs, speed, likelihood of execution and settlement, size, nature and any other consideration relevant to the execution of the order.

### Clause COBS-11.3.1R (Prompt and Fair Order Allocation)
A firm must execute client orders sequentially and promptly, ensuring that orders executed on behalf of clients are allocated fairly and without preference to proprietary trading books.
