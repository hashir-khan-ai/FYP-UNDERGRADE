# Financial Conduct Authority - Client Assets Sourcebook (CASS)

**Jurisdiction:** United Kingdom (FCA)  
**Document ID:** DOC-FCA-CASS-2024  
**Classification:** Public Regulation  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01  
**Permitted Roles:** ["analyst", "manager", "auditor"]  

---

## Chapter 6: Custody Rules

### Clause CASS-6.2.1R (Safe Custody and Asset Segregation)
A firm must, when holding safe custody assets belonging to clients, make adequate arrangements to safeguard clients' ownership rights and prevent the use of safe custody assets for its own account. A firm must ensure that custody assets are segregated from the firm's own proprietary assets on its books and records.

## Chapter 7: Client Money Rules

### Clause CASS-7.13.1R (Segregation of Client Money)
A firm must promptly place any client money received into one or more statutory segregated client bank accounts opened with an approved bank. Client money must never be commingled with the firm's own general corporate bank accounts.

### Clause CASS-7.15.2R (Daily Internal and External Reconciliations)
A firm must perform internal client money reconciliations every business day, comparing client ledger balances with client bank account balances. Any discrepancy between internal client records and physical bank balances must be investigated and corrected on the same business day, including funding any client money shortfall from the firm's own capital.
