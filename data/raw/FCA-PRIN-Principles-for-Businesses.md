## Chapter 2: The Principles for Businesses

### Clause PRIN-2.1.1R-P1 (Principle 1: Integrity)
A firm must conduct its business with integrity.

### Clause PRIN-2.1.1R-P2 (Principle 2: Skill, Care and Diligence)
A firm must conduct its business with due skill, care and diligence.

### Clause PRIN-2.1.1R-P3 (Principle 3: Management and Control)
A firm must take reasonable care to organise and control its affairs responsibly and effectively, with adequate risk management systems.

### Clause PRIN-2.1.1R-P6 (Principle 6: Customers' Interests)
A firm must pay due regard to the interests of its customers and treat them fairly.

### Clause PRIN-2.1.1R-P11 (Principle 11: Relations with Regulators)
A firm must deal with its regulators in an open and cooperative way, and must disclose to the FCA and PRA appropriately anything relating to the firm of which that regulator would reasonably expect notice.

### Clause PRIN-2.1.1R-P12 (Principle 12: Consumer Duty)
A firm must act to deliver good outcomes for retail customers. This overarching principle requires firms to proactively assess and demonstrate positive outcomes across product suitability, fair value, consumer understanding, and customer support.
