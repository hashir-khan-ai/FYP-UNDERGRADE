# Basel Committee on Banking Supervision - Fundamental Review of the Trading Book (FRTB)

**Jurisdiction:** Global (BCBS) / UK Implementation  
**Document ID:** DOC-BASEL-FRTB-2023  
**Classification:** Public Regulation  
**Effective Date:** 2023-01-01  
**Permitted Roles:** ["analyst", "manager", "auditor"]  

---

## Section 1: Boundary Between Banking Book and Trading Book

### Clause BASEL-FRTB-1.1 (Trading Book Boundary and Intent to Trade)
Instruments designated in the trading book must be held with trading intent, hedging other trading positions, or to benefit from short-term price movements. Any re-allocation between the banking book and trading book requires supervisory approval and cannot be executed to reduce regulatory capital requirements.

---

## Section 2: Standardised Approach for Market Risk

### Clause BASEL-FRTB-2.2 (Sensitivities-Based Method)
Under the Standardised Approach for market risk, capital requirements are determined using the sensitivities-based method across delta, vega, and curvature risks for interest rate, equity, foreign exchange, and commodity risk classes.

---

## Section 3: Default Risk Charge and Residual Risks

### Clause BASEL-FRTB-3.1 (Default Risk Charge - DRC)
Institutions must calculate a separate Default Risk Charge (DRC) to capture jump-to-default risk for debt and equity instruments held in the trading book that is not captured by market spread volatility.

### Clause BASEL-FRTB-4.1 (Residual Risk Add-On - RRAO)
Instruments with exotic underlying assets, multi-underlying correlation dependencies, or non-linear pay-offs that cannot be adequately captured by standard delta/vega risk charges are subject to an explicit Residual Risk Add-On (RRAO) capital charge.
