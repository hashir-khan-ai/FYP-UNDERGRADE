# Financial Conduct Authority - Dispute Resolution: Complaints (DISP)

**Jurisdiction:** United Kingdom (FCA)  
**Document ID:** DOC-FCA-DISP-2024  
**Classification:** Public Regulation  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01  
**Permitted Roles:** ["analyst", "manager", "auditor"]  

---

## Chapter 1: Treating Complainants Fairly

### Clause DISP-1.3.1R (Complaints Handling and Remediation Policies)
A firm must have in place and operate appropriate and effective procedures for handling complaints, which must be documented and published clearly on its website. A firm must ensure complaints are investigated competently, diligently and impartially, assessing whether the complaint should be upheld and what remedial action or redress is appropriate.

### Clause DISP-1.6.1R (Time Limits for Complaints Resolution)
On receipt of a complaint, a firm must send the complainant a prompt written acknowledgment. By the end of 8 weeks after receipt of the complaint, the firm must send the complainant either:
(1) a final response which accepts the complaint and offers redress, or rejects the complaint with clear reasons; or
(2) a written explanation why the firm is not in a position to make a final response, indicating when it expects to provide one and informing the complainant of their statutory right to refer the matter to the Financial Ombudsman Service (FOS).

---

## Chapter 2: Financial Ombudsman Service Jurisdiction

### Clause DISP-2.8.1R (Time Limits for Referral to Ombudsman)
The Ombudsman cannot consider a complaint if the complainant refers it to the Financial Ombudsman Service more than 6 months after the date on which the firm sent the complainant its final response, unless the firm has failed to provide required notice of FOS referral rights.
