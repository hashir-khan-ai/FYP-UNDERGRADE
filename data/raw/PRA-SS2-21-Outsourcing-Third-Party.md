# Prudential Regulation Authority - Supervisory Statement SS2/21: Outsourcing and Third Party Risk

**Jurisdiction:** United Kingdom (Bank of England / PRA)  
**Document ID:** DOC-PRA-SS221-2021  
**Classification:** Public Regulation  
**Effective Date:** 2022-03-31  
**Permitted Roles:** ["analyst", "manager", "auditor"]  

---

## Chapter 3: Critical and Material Outsourcing Classifications

### Clause PRA-SS221-3.1 (Materiality Assessment Criteria)
Before entering into any outsourcing agreement or cloud hosting contract, a firm must conduct a formal materiality assessment. An outsourcing arrangement is critical or material if a defect or failure in its performance would materially impair the firm's financial soundness, continuity of regulatory permissions, or compliance with regulatory standards.

### Clause PRA-SS221-3.4 (Notification of Material Outsourcing to Regulators)
A firm must formally notify the PRA before entering into or significantly amending any material outsourcing contract. Notification must be submitted in writing at least 30 business days before contract signing.

## Chapter 5: Written Agreements and Governance Safeguards

### Clause PRA-SS221-5.1 (Mandatory Contractual Safeguards)
Every material outsourcing contract must contain explicit terms guaranteeing:
(1) unrestricted audit, inspection, and access rights for the firm and regulators;
(2) strict information security and data encryption standards;
(3) sub-outsourcing restrictions requiring the firm's prior written consent;
(4) clear service level agreements (SLAs) with quantifiable performance metrics.

---

## Chapter 10: Exit Strategies and Business Continuity

### Clause PRA-SS221-10.2 (Comprehensive Exit and Transition Plans)
Firms must maintain documented, tested exit plans for all critical and material outsourcing arrangements. The exit plan must ensure that the firm can transition the outsourced activities to an alternative provider or bring them back in-house within an acceptable timeframe without breaching operational resilience impact tolerances.
