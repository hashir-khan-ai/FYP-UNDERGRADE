# Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) - Banking: Conduct of Business Sourcebook (BCOBS)

**Jurisdiction:** United Kingdom (FCA)  
**Document ID:** DOC-FCA-BCOBS-2024  
**Classification:** Public Regulation  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01  
**Permitted Roles:** ["analyst", "manager", "auditor"]  

---

## Chapter 2: Communications with Banking Customers

### Clause BCOBS-2.1.1R (Clear, Fair, and Not Misleading Communication)
A firm must ensure that any communication to a retail banking customer, or any financial promotion relating to a retail banking service, is fair, clear, and not misleading. This rule applies across all digital, printed, and spoken channels.

### Clause BCOBS-2.1.2G (Guidance on Clarity of Terms)
When presenting interest rates, fees, or charges to retail customers, a firm should prominently display annual equivalent rates (AER) alongside any gross rates. Explanations of fee calculation methods must be provided before agreement execution.

## Chapter 4: Information to be Provided to Customers

### Clause BCOBS-4.1.1R (Pre-Contract Information Disclosure)
A firm must provide a retail banking customer with pre-contract information within a reasonable time before the customer is bound by a retail banking contract. The disclosure must detail:
(1) all withdrawal restrictions, notice periods, and early termination penalties;
(2) all applicable account maintenance and service charges;
(3) the compensation arrangements under the Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS).

### Clause BCOBS-4.1.2R (Notification of Interest Rate Changes)
Where a firm reduces an interest rate on a deposit account with an account balance over £100, it must provide written notification to the customer at least 14 calendar days before the reduction takes effect. If the reduction is significant (exceeding 0.50% within a 12-month period), notification must be provided at least 30 calendar days in advance.

### Clause BCOBS-4.2.4R (Post-Contract Statements)
A firm must provide each payment account holder with an account statement at least once per month, without charge, in an accessible electronic or physical format.
