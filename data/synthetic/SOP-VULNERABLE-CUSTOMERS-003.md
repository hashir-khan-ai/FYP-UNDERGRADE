## Section 1: Identifying Drivers of Vulnerability

### Clause SOP-VULN-1.1 (Four Drivers of Vulnerability)
Frontline analysts and customer support teams must actively look out for signs of customer vulnerability across four key drivers:
(1) health conditions or cognitive impairments;
(2) major life events (such as bereavement, job loss, or relationship breakdown);
(3) low financial resilience or persistent debt distress;
(4) low financial or digital capability.

### Clause SOP-VULN-1.3 (BRRC Communication Protocol)
When speaking with potentially vulnerable customers, frontline staff must apply the BRRC communication protocol:
- Balance: adjust pace and tone of conversation;
- Repeat: reconfirm key information and fee obligations;
- Reframe: present complex terms in plain language;
- Check: verify mutual understanding before completing transactions.

## Section 2: Forbearance and Specialist Care Escalation

### Clause SOP-VULN-2.2 (Referral to Specialist Vulnerability Team)
Where a customer is experiencing severe cognitive decline, severe physical illness, or acute debt crises, frontline staff must offer an immediate referral to the bank's Specialist Customer Care Team within 1 business day.

### Clause SOP-VULN-2.4 (Explicit Consent for Vulnerability Recording)
Staff must obtain explicit customer consent before recording any health-related vulnerability indicators on the customer relationship management (CRM) database, in strict accordance with UK GDPR Article 9 special category data rules.
