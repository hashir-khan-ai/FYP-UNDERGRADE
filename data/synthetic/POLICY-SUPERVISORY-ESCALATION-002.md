## Section 1: Supervisory Breach Notification Matrix

### Clause POL-MGT-1.1 (Material Breach Notification Deadlines)
Compliance Managers must notify the PRA and FCA of any material regulatory breach or operational disruption within 24 hours of confirmation. Notification must be submitted via the formal FCA Connect notification portal.

### Clause POL-MGT-1.3 (Threshold for Senior Management Regime Escalation)
Any single compliance failure resulting in an unhedged financial exposure exceeding £500,000, or involving systemic customer detriment affecting more than 100 retail accounts, triggers mandatory formal notification to the designated Senior Management Function (SMF 16 - Compliance Oversight).

---

## Section 2: Management Remediation Protocol

### Clause POL-MGT-2.2 (Audit Remediation Timelines)
When an internal or external audit issue is assigned a 'High Severity' rating, the designated Compliance Manager must submit a formal Remediation Action Plan (RAP) to the Board Audit Committee within 14 business days. Corrective implementation must be fully completed within 60 calendar days of issue publication.

### Clause POL-MGT-2.5 (Disciplinary Reporting)
Compliance Managers must conduct a root-cause investigation within 10 business days for any repeated procedural omission committed by operational staff.
