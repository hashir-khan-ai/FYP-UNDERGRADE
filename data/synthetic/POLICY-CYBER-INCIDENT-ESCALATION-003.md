## Section 1: Severity Matrix and Supervisory Reporting

### Clause POL-CYBER-1.1 (Severity 1 Incident Criteria - Project Boreas Framework)
A cyber event is classified as a Severity 1 Critical Incident under the bank's internal 'Project Boreas' crisis framework if it involves unauthorized exfiltration of customer records exceeding 5,000 accounts, ransomware encryption of payment engines, or financial loss exceeding £750,000.

### Clause POL-CYBER-1.2 (Mandatory 4-Hour PRA/FCA Regulatory Notification)
For any confirmed Severity 1 incident, the designated Incident Manager must submit a formal notification to the PRA and FCA via the emergency supervisory hotline within 4 hours of confirmation, followed by an initial Incident Impact Assessment within 24 hours.

## Section 2: Ransomware Prohibitions and Post-Incident Remediation

### Clause POL-CYBER-2.3 (Strict Extortion and Ransomware Payment Ban)
The bank enforces a strict prohibition against negotiating with extortionists or making ransomware cryptocurrency payments. Any exception requires unanimous written authorization from the Board Risk Committee and notification to the National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC).

### Clause POL-CYBER-2.5 (Post-Incident Review and Lessons Learned Timelines)
Following incident containment, the Head of Operational Resilience must deliver a comprehensive Post-Incident Review (PIR) to the Executive Committee within 15 business days. All root-cause remediation items must be fully validated by Internal Audit within 90 calendar days.
