## Section 1: Trade Surveillance and STOR Submissions

### Clause POL-MAR-1.1 (Suspicious Transaction and Order Report - STOR Protocol)
Compliance Managers must submit a formal Suspicious Transaction and Order Report (STOR) to the FCA via the Connect portal without delay, and no later than 2 business days following the identification of reasonable suspicion of insider dealing, market manipulation, or attempted market abuse for orders exceeding €250,000.

### Clause POL-MAR-1.4 (Insider List Protocol 'Project Kronos')
Whenever the bank advises on confidential price-sensitive corporate transactions, the Compliance Manager must establish an Event-Based Insider List (designated internally under the 'Project Kronos' protocol). All listed individuals must provide written acknowledgment of their legal insider duties within 24 hours of list addition.

## Section 2: Personal Account Dealing (PAD) Pre-Clearance

### Clause POL-MAR-2.1 (Mandatory 7-Day PAD Pre-Clearance Window)
All material risk takers (MRTs) and surveillance managers must obtain written pre-clearance from the Compliance Department prior to executing any personal security transaction. Approved clearance remains valid for a maximum execution window of 7 business days.

### Clause POL-MAR-2.3 (Minimum 30-Day Holding Period)
Staff subject to PAD rules must hold approved financial instruments for a minimum holding period of 30 calendar days. Short-term speculative trading or short-selling of client securities is strictly forbidden and constitutes gross misconduct.
