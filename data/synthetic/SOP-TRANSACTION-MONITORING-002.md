## Section 1: Alert Triage and Investigation

### Clause SOP-TXM-1.1 (Transaction Monitoring Alert Triage SLA)
FinCrime analysts must review and disposition all automated transaction monitoring rule alerts within 5 business days of generation. Alerts generated by high-risk customer accounts or sudden transaction spikes exceeding £50,000 must be prioritized and dispositioned within 24 hours.

### Clause SOP-TXM-1.4 (Documenting False Positives)
When an alert is determined to be a false positive, the analyst must record clear operational rationale in the case management system, referencing verified source-of-funds documentation and historical customer profiling before closing the alert.

## Section 2: Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) Escalation

### Clause SOP-TXM-2.1 (Internal SAR Referral to Nominated Officer)
Where an analyst identifies red-flag indicators suggesting money laundering, terrorist financing, or proliferation financing, they must complete an Internal Suspicious Activity Report (ISAR) and submit it to the bank's Nominated Officer / Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO) within 2 business days.

### Clause SOP-TXM-2.3 (Tipping-Off Prohibition)
Under no circumstances may any staff member inform the customer, account beneficial owner, or any external third party that an alert has been triggered, an internal investigation is underway, or a SAR has been submitted to the National Crime Agency (NCA). Unauthorized disclosure constitutes criminal tipping-off.
