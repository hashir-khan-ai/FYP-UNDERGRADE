## Section 1: Retail Account Onboarding

### Clause SOP-KYC-1.1 (Identity Verification Standards)
Front-line analysts must verify the identity of individual retail applicants using at least one primary government-issued photographic document (valid passport, UK driving licence, or EEA national identity card) combined with one proof of residential address issued within the preceding three calendar months (utility bill or bank statement).

### Clause SOP-KYC-1.2 (Electronic Verification Matching)
Where onboarding is conducted digitally, electronic verification (e-IDV) must achieve a minimum 2+2 matching score (two verified full name matches and two verified address matches across independent credit reference bureau databases).

## Section 2: Corporate Customer Screening

### Clause SOP-KYC-2.1 (Ultimate Beneficial Ownership Verification)
Analysts must obtain certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, and register of members for corporate entities. All individuals possessing directly or indirectly greater than 25% ownership or voting rights must undergo full KYC identity verification.

### Clause SOP-KYC-2.4 (Sanctions and Adverse Media Screening)
Every corporate applicant, its directors, and beneficial owners must be screened against the UK HM Treasury OFSI Consolidated List, UN Sanctions List, and EU Sanctions List prior to account activation. Any potential positive hit requires immediate suspension of the onboarding workflow.
